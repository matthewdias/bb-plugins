Triaging threads on a phone means opening each one's menu. Swipe Controls
lets you swipe them instead, as you would mail: right to mark read, left to
pin or archive. On the desktop app, two fingers across the page go back and
forward.

## What you get

**Swipe a thread right** to mark it read, or unread if it already is.

**Swipe it left** to uncover Pin and Archive. Let go past halfway and the row
stays open on them. Swipe all the way and it archives when you let go, with
bb's own Undo toast.

**Two fingers across the page** go back and forward in bb's desktop app. An
arrow slides in from the edge and fills as you swipe. A code block that can
still scroll sideways keeps the swipe.

**Haptic ticks** in bb's iOS and Android apps: one as a swipe reaches the
point where letting go acts, and a firmer one when a full swipe archives.
Android browsers get short vibrations. Trackpads and iOS Safari can't play
haptics from a web page, so they get none.

Works with a finger on a phone and two fingers on a trackpad, with bb's thread
list or any list that replaces it.

## On a phone

bb closes its sidebar with a leftward swipe. By default, a leftward swipe that
starts on a thread swipes the thread instead, and the sidebar closes from its
header, empty space, or a tap beside it. One setting turns that off, and then
threads only swipe right.

## Settings

Turn off row swipes, the phone's leftward swipe, the full-swipe archive, or
haptics. Choose where back and forward work: in the desktop app (the
default, since browsers already have it), always, or never.
