Triaging threads on a phone means opening each one's menu. Swipe Controls
lets you swipe them instead, as you would mail: right to mark read, left for
the actions you keep on bb's thread rows. On the desktop app, two fingers across the page go back and
forward.

## What you get

**Swipe a thread right** to mark it read, or unread if it already is. A
setting makes that any of bb's own thread actions instead.

**Swipe it left** to uncover the row actions you picked under bb's Customize
row actions, in the same order: Pin, Archive, Rename, Open in split, Copy
link, or another plugin's action. Each does what bb's menu does. Let go past
halfway and the row stays open on them. Move to section is left out, since
it has to ask which section.

**Swipe all the way left** and it archives when you let go, with bb's own
Undo toast. That too is a setting, and needn't be one of your row actions. On a
trackpad it takes a deliberate swipe, not a flick.

bb's own row buttons are hidden while you swipe threads, so titles get their
room. A setting brings them back.

Needs bb 0.46 or later.

**Two fingers across the page** go back and forward in bb's desktop app. An
arrow slides in from the edge and fills as you swipe. A code block that can
still scroll sideways keeps the swipe.

**Haptic ticks** in bb's iOS and Android apps: one as a swipe reaches the
point where letting go acts, and a firmer one when a full swipe archives.
Android browsers get short vibrations. Trackpads and iOS Safari can't play
haptics from a web page, so they get none.

Works with a finger on a phone and two fingers on a trackpad, with bb's thread
list or any list that replaces it.

## On a phone

bb closes its sidebar with a leftward swipe. By default, a leftward swipe that
starts on a thread swipes the thread instead, and the sidebar closes from its
header, empty space, or a tap beside it. One setting turns that off, and then
threads only swipe right.

## Settings

Turn off row swipes, the phone's leftward swipe, or haptics. Choose what a
swipe right and a swipe all the way left do, and whether bb's own row buttons
stay. Choose where back and forward work: in the desktop app (the
default, since browsers already have it), always, or never.
