When an agent asks you something, bb puts the question above the composer and
lets it grow to the height of the chat. A long question with long options
covers the conversation it is asking about, and scrolls inside a scroller
inside the chat. Question Dock moves the card out of the way. It is bb's own
card, with its keyboard shortcuts and multi-question tabs intact.

## What you get

**Docked beside the chat.** On a wide window the card takes a column on the
right of the thread, and the chat and composer move over for it. You can
read back through the conversation while you answer.

**Floating where you put it.** Drag the card's header and it floats over the
chat, staying above the composer. It stays where you drop it, on that device,
across reloads. Drag it to the right edge to dock it again; a dashed outline
shows where it will land. A window too narrow to dock floats the card
instead.

**A sheet on your phone.** The card rises from the bottom of the screen over
a dimmed chat. Swipe its handle to make it taller or shorter. Tap the chat to
put it away as bb's own bar above the composer, and tap the bar to bring it
back.

**One scroller.** However it's placed, the card is sized for the space it
has, and its options scroll on their own.

Plan reviews and plugin forms (Grill, for one) use the same card and move
with it. Approvals stay beside the composer, where you want them.
