bb puts every destination in its navigation, beside the threads. Open GitHub
and your thread is gone. Go back and GitHub forgets the pull request you were
reading. Tab Nav turns bb's rail into tabs. Keep several open, switch between
them, and each one comes back where you left it.

## What you get

**Threads is a tab.** It is first, it never closes, and it is bb as you know
it: the thread list and the thread in view. It counts what is waiting on you
and what is running, and opens a switcher on hover. The switcher lists what
needs you, what's running, what finished and where you were, one click from
each.

**Destinations open below it.** Open plugin panels, Plugins and Skills from
the + button, or reach one any other way and it gets a tab. Drag to reorder,
middle-click to close, Ctrl+Shift+T to reopen.

**Narrow or wide.** Icons only, at bb's own rail width, or a wider rail with
names. A toggle at the rail's foot switches, and the thread list keeps its
width either way.

**Each tab returns to where you left it.** Each tab remembers the exact page
it was on: the pull request inside GitHub, not just GitHub.

**The sidebar gets out of the way.** On a destination the thread list folds
away to the rail, so the page has the window, and returns with Threads. Each
tab keeps it as you left it there.

**Pinned tabs.** Pin the destinations you always want: they sit just below
Threads and never close by accident. A pin remembers where you pinned it. A
dot marks one you've moved away from, and closing it with bb's Close or the
keyboard sends it back there and leaves it pinned.

**Badges and splits.** A panel's badge shows on its tab, as on bb's Rail.
Drag a tab out into the page to open it in a split with bb's own drag, or
⌘-click it. Tabs on screen in a split are marked.

**Keyboard.** Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab switch tabs, and every command is
in the palette.

## How it works

Tab Nav is one of bb's navigations, drawn in the rail column bb lays out, so
it needs bb 0.47.1 or later. Choose it under Settings → Appearance →
Navigation, or leave that on Automatic. Choose another navigation and Tab Nav
steps aside entirely. Tabs are per device.

The wide rail widens bb's rail through a CSS variable bb doesn't promise; if
bb changes it, the rail stays narrow.

On a phone the sidebar is a drawer, and it shows bb's own Rail.

Tab Nav was called Top Tabs until 0.5, when its tabs ran across the top of the
window.
