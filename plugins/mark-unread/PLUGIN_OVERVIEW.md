You read halfway down a thread and get pulled away. bb can mark the whole
thread unread, but when you come back it starts you at the top. Mark Unread
works like Slack: pick the message where you stopped, and the thread shows as
unread with a New line above that message. When you come back, it scrolls you
there.

## What you get

**Mark from any message.** Choose Mark unread from here in a message's action
bar, or hold Option and click the message. The thread gets bb's own unread dot
in the sidebar.

**A New line where you stopped.** The line is drawn like bb's own, in your
theme's colour, above the message you picked. Your own messages are never
unread to you, so marking one puts the line above the reply.

**Back where you left off.** Opening the thread scrolls straight to the line,
even when it is far up a long thread. Leaving again forgets the point, as
Slack does once you have seen it.

**Your choice of key.** Option, Command or Shift, or switch the click off and
keep the action bar. Points are kept on the server, so they follow you across
windows.

## Good to know

bb's API can only mark a whole thread unread, so the line and the
modifier-click rely on bb's timeline markup, which a future bb release may
change. The action-bar entry and the unread dot do not depend on it.
